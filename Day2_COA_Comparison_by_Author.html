Where the team agrees

  • Deadline and principles. All 14 carry the 2359, 30 November deadline and the President’s four principles. None disputes that any early invasion must be thwarted.

  • A forward-versus-depth axis. Six pairs are forward denial against standoff denial (Allen, Beach, Isabelle, Jernigan, Robles, Taylor). Cooper, West and Mastromauro share the first half and change the second.

  • Forward denial as the baseline. Six recommend a forward COA 1 and fold COA 2 in as support (Beach, Cooper, Isabelle, Jernigan, Robles, Taylor). Rariden and Wilson recommend posture first, response on attack.

  • Undersea forces first. Allen, Beach, Coates, Isabelle, Jernigan, Mastromauro, Stavrov and Taylor put the 10 SSNs and 1 SSGN on the gates and the Penghu Channel as the early denial force.

  • Disperse, harden, front-load. ACE dispersal, IAMD shifted to Guam and Okinawa, and supplies moved to Taiwan before closure appear in 11 or more inputs.

  • Munitions relief. A 30-day relief request to CENTCOM and EUCOM for interceptors and anti-ship munitions: Allen, Beach, Coates, Jernigan, Mastromauro, Rariden, Taylor, Wilson.

  • No U.S. mainland strikes. Beach, Coates (no mainland invasion), Mastromauro, Stavrov (for U.S. forces) and Taylor (as an escalation risk). Nobody proposes U.S. forces invading.

  • Release verified ISR; ask allies, with dates. Japan, the Philippines and Australia carry the access asks. South Korea stays on the Peninsula. Access is treated as unconfirmed by most authors.

  • Wargame before committing. Robles, Rariden, West and Taylor tie the choice to wargaming, Red Team and confirmed access and authorities.

Where it differs

Topic Positions (who)
What 30 November does NCA decision point with a COA ready to execute (Beach, Rariden). Automatic kinetic sea denial on expiry or on an early PLA launch (Taylor; Mastromauro’s Phase III is similar). Combat from 6 Nov (Stavrov). Not a combat trigger; response needs an actual attack (Rariden’s Approach 2; Wilson’s COA 1 stays defensive until escalation).
Strike boundary No mainland strikes (Beach base, Mastromauro, Taylor, Coates). Limited strikes on launch sites that are firing (Beach COA 1 draft). Taiwan strikes mainland ports with U.S. targeting data (Stavrov). Cyber attack on PRC financial system (Cooper).
The PRC zone Operate inside, refuse the 144-hour approval (Smith COA 2, Stavrov, Beach). Stay outside or avoid encounters (Smith COA 1, Rariden, Coates presence, Taylor COA 2).
East-coast lifeline (Hualien, Suao) Treated as reachable and uncontested (Allen, Coates, Isabelle, Mastromauro, Stavrov, Taylor). Beach notes the Intel Map puts Taiwan’s east coast inside the zone.
U.S. presence on Taiwan Limited air and missile defense, ISR, maritime and advisors with consent (Beach). SOF, liaison or AIT only (Cooper, Coates, Taylor COA 2). None (Mastromauro, Isabelle, Allen, Rariden).
How hard to hit the PLA Lift and blockade forces only (Beach, Isabelle, Allen). Lift plus PLAAF and PLAN surface groups, 60–120 days (Mastromauro COA 2). Whole fleet including the Liaoning (Stavrov COA 2).
Force levels Day 1 posture: 10 SSNs and 1 SSGN, one CSG forward (Beach, Allen, Taylor). 2 to 3 CSGs, 12 SSNs, 2 SSGNs, 18 bombers (Mastromauro). Draw forces from other theaters (Cooper).
Contact with Beijing Continuous engagement and a State-owned off-ramp (Smith COA 1, West, Robles, Coates, Beach). No PRC contact until they stand down (Smith COA 2).
Time horizon 24 days (Stavrov COA 1), 18 days (Stavrov COA 2), 30–45 days (Allen COA 1, Taylor), 45–90 days (Allen COA 2), 60–120 days (Mastromauro COA 2), sustain past 30 Nov (Rariden).
Sustainment NDRF and MSC sealift bridge (Stavrov). Littoral shuttle from Darwin and Palau (Allen, Taylor). Reserve fleet sealift (Mastromauro). Hub-and-spoke from Guam, Pearl Harbor and Australia (Beach, Coates).
Centers of gravity Landing force and enforcement force, 7th Fleet (Group CES; most authors). PLAN Eastern Fleet as the enemy COG (Taylor).
Quantitative modeling Monte Carlo odds (>95% success for COA 1, >90% failure for COA 2) and a day-by-day dashboard (Taylor). Everyone else is qualitative.
